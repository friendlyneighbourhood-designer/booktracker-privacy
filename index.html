Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Book Tracker: Reading Log ("we", "our", or "the App") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Local Personal Reading Data: All books, reading sessions, pages logged, reading streaks, quotes, and custom shelves you create are saved locally on your device in a private SQLite/Room database. We do not transmit or store your personal library data on external servers.

Camera Access: The App requests camera permission solely to scan ISBN barcodes on physical books. Barcode scanning is processed on-device. Images from your camera are never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Services:

  • Google Books API: Used solely to look up public book metadata (title, author, cover, page count) when you search or scan an ISBN barcode. No personal user identification is shared with Google Books API.
  • In-App Purchases / Subscriptions: If you purchase premium features, transactions are processed directly by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We do not collect or store your payment card numbers or billing addresses.

2. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your reading history is stored on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can edit or delete any book, shelf, or quote at any time within the app, or delete all data completely by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

3. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
